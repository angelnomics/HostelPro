🛡️ Security Reporting Guidelines

  • Report security incidents as soon as possible.
  • Provide accurate information about what happened.
  • Include the hostel, room and exact location whenever possible.
  • Record people involved only when their identities are known.
  • Mark an incident as critical when immediate intervention is required.
  • Keep investigation and resolution information updated.

🚨 Emergency Response

For immediate danger, violence, fire or another emergency, follow the hostel's emergency procedures and contact the appropriate emergency services or responsible hostel staff immediately.
